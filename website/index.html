Features

Built for people who actually run brokers.

Every screen is designed around the workflow of debugging and operating real MQTT deployments — not a toy demo client.

▣

Client certificates & mTLS

Import CA certs, client certs and keys from PEM, paste them directly, or unlock a .p12 bundle in one step — full mutual TLS, no laptop required.

≋

Live message feed

Watch subscribed topics stream in real time, expand any message for QoS, retain flag and payload, and re-send or edit-and-send instantly.

▤

Publish composer

Compose JSON, text, or Base64 payloads with topic autocomplete drawn from your history, and pin frequent sends as snippets.

⌁

TCP, TLS, WS & WSS

Full MQTT 3.1.1 and 5.0 support over every standard transport, with per-client keep-alive, clean-session, and timeout controls.

⏣

Smart topic suggestions

Subscriptions and publishes both surface topics you've used before on that client, so you're never retyping long topic paths.

◆

Brokers & clients, kept apart

Save a broker's host, port and transport once, then create as many clients against it as you need — each with its own ID, auth and certs.

Security & privacy

Your credentials never leave your device.

There's no account, no backend, and nothing phoning home. MQTT Connect only talks to the brokers your clients connect to.

✓
Encrypted at rest

Broker profiles, clients, usernames, passwords and certificates are held in the OS keychain and encrypted storage — never in plain text.

✓
Offline, no telemetry

No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters phoning a server, no usage tracking of any kind.

✓
Face ID & auto-lock

Gate the app behind biometrics, auto-lock after inactivity, and hide payload previews in the app switcher.

✓
Certificates stay put

Client certificates and private keys are read once through the system file picker and never included in client export.
